# Coffee_Shop_Sales_Analysis
Analyzed 149,116 coffee shop transactions using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify sales trends, top-performing products, store performance, and peak sales hours. Calculated revenue, order volume, and month-over-month growth, and created KPI measures to support data-driven decisions on inventory planning, staffing, and sales performance.
Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

# Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and evaluate product and store performance. Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, I explored transaction data to identify patterns in revenue, order volume, product demand, and sales across different time periods.

# Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.

2. Track monthly sales growth, order volume, and quantity sold.

3. Identify top-performing product categories and product types.

4. Compare sales performance across store locations.

5. Analyze hourly and daily sales patterns to identify peak demand periods.

# Tools & Technologies

SQL: Data analysis, aggregations, CTEs, window functions, and month-over-month comparisons.

Python: Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and feature engineering using Pandas.

Power BI: KPI reporting, DAX measures, and sales performance visualization.

Excel: Data review and supporting analysis.

# Key Analysis

Evaluated revenue, order volume, and quantity sold over time.

Analyzed month-over-month sales performance.

Compared product categories, product types, and store locations.

Investigated sales patterns by hour, day, and month.

Explored forecasting and predictive modelling as extensions of the analysis.

# Business Value

The analysis helps highlight sales trends, high-performing products, and periods of high demand. These insights can support better-informed decisions around inventory planning, staffing, and sales performance monitoring.

# Project Outcome

Developed a sales analysis workflow combining SQL-based business analysis, Python-based data exploration, and Power BI KPI reporting to turn raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.
